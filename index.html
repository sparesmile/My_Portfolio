01 — About

The person behind the data

I'm a Data Analyst with a strong background in the Master Data domain, specializing in Power BI, MS Excel, and data visualization. My work focuses on translating business requirements into technical solutions and maintaining data governance at scale.

My process is simple: understand the stakeholder's need, reconcile the data, and deliver a report that improves efficiency. I've consistently improved reporting efficiency by 25% to 30% through collaboration and process standardization.

I have hands-on experience managing tickets within SLAs, creating reporting dashboards, performing Gap Analysis and Root Cause Analysis, and collaborating with SMEs to support implementation projects.

Without data, you're just another person with an opinion.
